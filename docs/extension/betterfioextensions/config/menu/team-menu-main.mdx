# teamMenu.yml

`menu/Team Menu/teamMenu.yml` is the main team hub for players who already belong to a BetterTeams team.

## YAML format

```yaml
DEFAULT:
  title: "<black><bold>| <dark_gray>TEAM MENU"
  rows: 6
  items: {}

ADMIN:
  title: "<black><bold>| <dark_gray>TEAM MENU"
  rows: 6
  items: {}

OWNER:
  title: "<black><bold>| <dark_gray>TEAM MENU"
  rows: 6
  items: {}
```

## How to use

Place normal member buttons under `DEFAULT`, staff controls under `ADMIN`, and owner-only controls under `OWNER`. Use `[OPEN_MENU]` for other menus, `[PLAYER]` for BetterTeams commands, and `[BETTERFIOEXTENSIONS] TEAM_LOGO` or `TEAM_INVITE_PROMPT` for extension behavior.
