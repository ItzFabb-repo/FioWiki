# BetterFioExtensions

## What the extension actually includes

### BetterTeams menu layer

BetterFioExtensions adds a GUI-first control layer around BetterTeams. It opens different menus depending on whether the player:

- has no team
- is a normal team member
- is a team admin
- is a team owner

### Main team systems

The bundled source includes menus and handlers for:

- creating a team
- browsing public teams
- accepting team invitations
- sending and managing join requests
- opening a role-aware team menu
- viewing team members and managing members
- team bank deposits, withdrawals, and transaction logs
- team activity logs
- team levels and upgrade previews
- saved home and anchor locations
- team warps
- ally request management
- team color and glow selection
- team head-banner styling
- per-team permission toggles

### Storage and integrations

The extension depends on BetterTeams and can optionally use PlaceholderAPI and Vault. It also ships storage support for:

- YAML
- SQLite
- MySQL

Runtime data is stored separately for join requests, team banners, team permissions, saved locations, team bank transactions, and team logs.
